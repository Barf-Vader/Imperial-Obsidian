### 4E cognition
Embodied - shape by the body, Embedded - shaped by environment, Enactive - something an agent does, Extended - partly outside the head.
- Embodied - the ear approximately performs a fourier transform mechanically. well designed body leaves less work for the brain
- Embedded - to understand behaviour we must understand the agent and the world
- Enactive - Perception and action.
- Extended - Action that you take to uncover info that is hidden or hard to compute mentally (rotating pieces in tetris, phone)
